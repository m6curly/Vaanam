QA_PIXEL masks fill, dilated cloud, cirrus, cloud, cloud shadow and snow. ST_QA is read with its 0.01 K scale when supplied. Large scenes are read and processed in 128-row tiles without a fixed pixel-count or 500 MB upload cap; actual limits depend on browser/device memory and available storage.

For a defensible result, upload the matching QA_PIXEL file. Without it, the calculation cannot identify cloud, shadow or snow pixels and the output will be marked unmasked.
